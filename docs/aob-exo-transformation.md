## 2. Draft MTP — three-layer protocol

**Candidate MTP:** *"A breath away from anyone's best state."* (Working line; protocol is load-bearing.)

- **Constraint Layer:** never make/imply a health/medical claim or advise a contraindicated participant (pregnancy, cardiovascular, epilepsy); never alter certification status, pricing, or program structure autonomously; never contact leads outside consented channels or exfiltrate the contact DB; never represent an uncertified person as a certified facilitator.
- **Decision Layer:** brand/safety integrity > speed > volume; facilitator quality (satisfaction-by-facilitator) > raw certification throughput; retention ≥ acquisition when resources are scarce.
- **Identity Layer:** AOB is a community of practice; agents protect facilitators' time for human connection, not replace it.
- **Litmus tests:** (1) provisional pass for triage/scheduling; (2) pass once the Constraint Layer is signed.

## 6. REWRITE Playbook — six steps tailored to AOB

- **Step 1 BACKCAST & DEFINE** *(gate: Five Design Conditions)* — one-day founder workshop; sign Destination Architecture + mandate. Conditions today: AI-centric workflow = not yet; recursive improvement = partial; model sovereignty = not yet; intelligence density = not measured; human flourishing = strong intent. **Not complete until conditions 1, 3, 4 instantiated.**
- **Step 2 ASSESS & PREPARE** — Readiness Score (§7); stand up MVIS week one; choose on-ramp.
- **Step 3 EXTRACT** *(gate: Workflow Data Manifest)* — elicitation agent first; sign a one-page Manifest for the first workflow. Binary rule: no stated reason, no field.
- **Step 4 DIAGNOSE & STRIP** — Task Decomposition Matrix across Admin/Operations; disposition agent_now / pilot / stay_human. CAIO = a hat Malik wears.
- **Step 5 BUILD & PROVE** *(parallel run as shadow mode + four cold-start feeds)* — historical replay, shadow comparison (correlation ID), human-correction capture, synthetic edge cases. Test of a real twin: override rate falls. Budget 10–15% of savings for the People Side.
- **Step 6 REWIRE & EVOLVE** — pod-based ops; Continuous Kill Switch as rhythm; measure Organizational Half-Life.

## 8. Smallest safe first workflow
**Wave 1: Facilitator Certification-Pipeline Coordination & Renewal-Risk Detection** — high coordination-to-judgment ratio, high-volume, rule-clear, measurable, reversible, low regulatory exposure, historical cases available, Fiduciary Wedge respected (pricing/status decisions/disputes stay human). Runner-up: membership access-sync + support triage. NOT first: pricing/program changes, certification pass/fail, dispute resolution, medical screening. Benchmarks: cycle time, reminder on-time rate, admin hours/cohort, access-sync error rate, and — above all — **override rate must fall** (target: halve in 60 days).
